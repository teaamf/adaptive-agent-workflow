# Adaptive Agent Workflow

Distinguish executing a project from reviewing its workflow or prompt. Embedded examples do not authorize execution.

Choose one loading tier first: [COMPACT_PROMPT.md](COMPACT_PROMPT.md) for small-context or quick work; [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), [AGENT_INTERACTION.md](AGENT_INTERACTION.md) and [PROJECT_TEMPLATE.md](PROJECT_TEMPLATE.md) for modular use; or the self-contained [MASTER_PROMPT.md](MASTER_PROMPT.md) when file access is unavailable and the context fits. Use the fast path for small reversible tasks. Do not load all tiers.

Load only relevant methods: [PROFILES.md](PROFILES.md) for domain work, [UI_DESIGN_PROTOCOL.md](UI_DESIGN_PROTOCOL.md) for human-facing design, [UI_REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.md](UI_REVIEW_PLAYBOOK.md) for material visual refinement, and [CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md](CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md) for workflow revision. [HOST_ADAPTERS.md](HOST_ADAPTERS.md) maps capabilities; [WORKFLOW_CONFIGURATION.md](WORKFLOW_CONFIGURATION.md) covers rule placement, verified enforcement and skill packaging during adoption. [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) explains use and maintenance.

Load [PRACTICAL_PLAYBOOKS.md](PRACTICAL_PLAYBOOKS.md) when concrete role packets, scenario procedures, evidence fields or optional integration steps are useful. They remain subordinate to the core's assurance and applicability rules. Do not treat a structural checker result or synthetic passing report as supported acceptance.

Preserve project scope and permissions. Use the smallest eligible team and declared assurance profile. Do not inherit a product, platform, fixed tracer count, clean-room rule or mandatory certification from an example. First-party work cannot satisfy a required independent review. Missing capability limits claims; it does not justify invented evidence or silently waived gates.
