# Supplied design-advice index

All 450 advice titles are indexed for traceability, not mandated as 450 gates. ED = Emotional Design (128); DI = Designing Interfaces (122); UP = Universal Principles of Design (200). Numbers follow the supplied documents, not a claim that the original books used these lists. Agentic interpretations belong to the supplied analyses.

Operational synthesis: UI_DESIGN_PROTOCOL U1–U2 covers goals/context and exploration; U3–U4 covers structure/patterns/states; U5–U6 covers measurement and accessibility; U7 covers emotional quality and ethical limits; U8 and CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT cover bias, evaluation and resource discipline. From version 4.2:

- U9 covers AI-enabled product features.
- AGENT_INTERACTION applies the same principles to the agent's own behavior toward people: legible state, questions, checkpoints, resumption, trust calibration and failure reports.
- WORKFLOW §11 turns the bias principles (UP007, UP034, UP042, UP085, UP114, UP132, UP138, UP145, UP166, UP177, UP179, UP181) into judgment checks.

Bracketed IDs in those modules cite this index. Individual principles remain optional, conditional or rejected where their assumptions do not fit. Inclusion in this index is not an endorsement.

See [RESEARCH_AND_CHANGES.md](../RESEARCH_AND_CHANGES.md) for the source-level adoption and qualification rationale. The index intentionally does not reproduce the books or all paraphrased advice. Original supplied filenames identify provenance; they are not runtime dependencies.
