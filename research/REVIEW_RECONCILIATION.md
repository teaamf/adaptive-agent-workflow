## What reconciliation could not do

- Have the reconciled candidate reviewed by a different model family or an enforced boundary. A second review (below) ran in the same family under cooperative separation.
- Meet the compact-size target without dropping 4.3 safeguards (R-17). The author accepted about 10.2 KB provisionally (10,239 bytes now); the owner may overturn it.
- Make the A5 ablation remove every mention of review. By definition it removes requirements, not the honesty rules that hold in every arm (EXPERIMENT_CONTRACT "Ablation validity").
